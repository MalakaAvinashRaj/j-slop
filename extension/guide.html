J-Slop filters low-value LinkedIn writing with Jev. Reveal any post, tune your threshold, and keep API spending small.

Free extensionYour OpenRouter keyNo local server

Start in three steps.

01

Add your key

Open J-Slop from Chrome's extensions menu. Expand OpenRouter connection, paste your OpenRouter API key, and click Save key. Use a key with credits and access to TypeSafe Jev.

02

Open your feed

Refresh your LinkedIn feed and scroll. Posts stay visible while being checked. If a score crosses your threshold, the post collapses once.

03

Make it yours

Choose Show post to reveal a filtered post. That choice lasts for the page session. Lower the threshold to filter more; raise it to be more selective. Toggle Clear the noise to pause.

Free to install.
API usage costs extra.

You pay OpenRouter directly for classifications. J-Slop does not include free API credits.

  • At most 1,500 UTF-8 bytes from each post.
  • Beginning, middle and ending samples for long posts.
  • Decisions cached locally for 30 days.
  • Slider changes reuse saved scores.
  • 250 new API attempts per UTC day, including failures.

If something doesn't work.

No posts checked? Refresh LinkedIn after installing or updating. Make sure filtering is enabled and your key is saved.

Provider restriction? Allow TypeSafe and Jev in your OpenRouter key's guardrail.

Request failed? Check the message at the bottom of the feed. Fix the key, credits or provider settings, then refresh LinkedIn. Failed API requests do not automatically retry.

Daily limit reached? New requests become available after midnight UTC. Refresh your feed then. Previously cached decisions can still be reused.

Your data, clearly explained.

Small post excerpts go directly from your extension to OpenRouter and TypeSafe. Your key, settings, decision hashes and probabilities stay in local extension storage. J-Slop runs no developer collection server and includes no ads or analytics.

API keys are not encrypted by J-Slop. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to access them. Remove key clears your saved key; uninstalling removes local extension data. External providers may retain records under their own policies.

Scores judge writing style, not AI authorship. Excerpts can miss context. J-Slop reads text; it does not read text inside images or videos. Independent project; no affiliation with LinkedIn, OpenRouter or TypeSafe.
